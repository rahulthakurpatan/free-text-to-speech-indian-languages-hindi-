About the project

About Vāṇī AI

Making professional voice generation more accessible to everyone.

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Why this project began

A simpler way to create voice content

Vāṇī AI Studio began with a straightforward idea: creating voice content should be easier to explore, even if someone does not have recording equipment, a studio, or experience with audio software. I wanted to build a practical tool that helps people turn written words into spoken audio through a clear and approachable workflow.

What the tool does

Text-to-speech for everyday projects

Vāṇī AI provides a browser-based text-to-speech interface for people who want to create narration and audio from written content. It may be useful for learning materials, presentations, videos, explainers, and personal creative projects. Available voices, languages, formats, and usage limits depend on the current tool and its connected AI service.

Why I built Vāṇī AI

My name is Rahul Singh Thakur. I am the founder of Vāṇī AI Studio, an independent digital project based in Patan, Madhya Pradesh, India.

I started Vāṇī AI because I wanted to make professional-style voice generation more approachable for creators, students, educators, freelancers, and small businesses. I was interested in how newer AI tools could reduce the effort involved in producing spoken content and make the process available to more people.

I developed this website with the assistance of AI-powered coding tools. They supported parts of the development process, but the project direction, product decisions, testing, content, and ongoing improvements are part of my own independent work. Vāṇī AI is a continuing project, and I work to improve it based on practical use and feedback.

How the service works

A browser interface with a BYOK model

Vāṇī AI uses a Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) approach for its connected AI voice-generation service. Users provide their own supported API key and use it through the Vāṇī AI interface. The key is saved in the user's browser storage to support subsequent use on that browser.

When a user requests audio generation, the browser sends the relevant request to the third-party AI provider using the supplied key. The provider processes the request and returns the generated audio. Vāṇī AI provides the interface; it does not operate or control the third-party AI provider.

Third-party providers may apply their own pricing, free-tier quotas, rate limits, data practices, availability conditions, and terms. These may change independently. Users should review the provider's current documentation and terms before using their API key.

Privacy and responsible use

We want visitors to understand what happens when they use the tool. Because Vāṇī AI connects to a third-party AI service, text submitted for generation is sent to that provider for processing. The API key is stored in browser storage, so users should protect access to their device and browser and should not use a shared or public device for sensitive work.

Please do not submit confidential, personal, or sensitive information unless you have considered the relevant risks and the third-party provider's policies. For more information about website data practices, cookies, analytics, and third-party services, read our Privacy Policy.

Users are responsible for having the necessary rights and permissions for the text they submit and for using generated audio lawfully. Please read our Terms and Conditions before using the service.

Who can use a text-to-speech tool?

Written information is useful, but not everyone prefers to read every piece of content on a screen. Audio can offer another way to experience information, review a script, prepare narration, or make a digital project more engaging. A text-to-speech tool can help people explore that option without arranging a recording session for every small task.

Vāṇī AI is intended for a broad range of everyday creative and practical uses. A video creator may want to test narration for a draft. A teacher or student may want to listen to prepared learning material. A freelancer or small business may need a voice track for a presentation, product explanation, or internal demonstration. Someone working on a personal project may simply want to hear how a paragraph sounds when spoken aloud.

The tool is not a replacement for every professional recording workflow. For projects that require a specific human performance, specialist voice direction, broadcast production, or guaranteed pronunciation, users should review the generated audio carefully and decide whether it meets their needs.

AI-assisted coding has been part of how I built the website. These tools helped me explore implementation options, write and revise code, and work through development problems. I still need to make decisions about what the website should do, review the output, test changes, and take responsibility for the project. Using AI during development does not mean every generated suggestion is automatically correct, so testing and revision remain important parts of the process.

I also believe product descriptions should be realistic. Features can depend on the connected provider, the user's API access, browser capabilities, and the current version of the website. If an option is unavailable or a provider changes its service, the experience may change too. I aim to describe the service in a way that helps visitors understand these dependencies instead of promising results that cannot be guaranteed.

Understanding the BYOK model

BYOK stands for “Bring Your Own Key.” In this model, a user obtains an API key from a supported third-party provider and enters it into the website to access that provider's service. The user remains responsible for obtaining the key appropriately, keeping it private, and understanding any charges, quotas, or restrictions attached to the provider account.

An API key should be treated like a password. Do not share it with other people, publish it in screenshots or public posts, or enter it on a device you do not trust. If you believe a key has been exposed, follow the provider's instructions to revoke or replace it. Browser storage is convenient, but it should not be treated as a secure password vault.

The third-party provider, not Vāṇī AI, determines its own API billing, account eligibility, model availability, usage limits, and data-handling terms. Please check those details directly with the provider before using the service, especially for business or sensitive projects.

Audio quality, review, and user responsibility

AI-generated speech can be useful, but results may vary with the language, spelling, punctuation, text length, selected voice, model, and provider-side changes. Names, specialist terms, abbreviations, numbers, and regional expressions may not always be pronounced as intended. For that reason, generated audio should be listened to and checked before it is published, shared, or used in an important setting.

Users are responsible for checking that they have permission to use the text they submit and that their final audio complies with applicable laws, platform rules, and the rights of other people. Do not use voice-generation tools to impersonate someone, mislead listeners, violate privacy, or create content that infringes another person's rights.

Vāṇī AI is designed as a practical creative utility. It does not provide legal, medical, financial, or other professional advice, and generated speech should not be treated as verified factual content. Review the underlying text and audio for accuracy before relying on them.
