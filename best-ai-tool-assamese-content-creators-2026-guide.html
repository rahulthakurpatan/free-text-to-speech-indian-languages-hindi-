4. How Assamese Creators Make AI Narration Sound Human

Naturalness is not a premium feature you buy; it is an acoustic system you design inside your script. Spoken Assamese relies heavily on local sentence rhythms, soft sibilant shifts, and breathing pauses: - **Punctuation Pause Engineering:** Stop writing long, run-on sentences. Use periods (.) frequently to force the AI engine to drop its pitch naturally (downstep), signaling authority. Insert **double commas (,,)** before delivering important facts or answers to create a natural, human-like thinking pause. - **Phonetic Transliteration Hacks:** If the neural voice struggles with a local name or technical English word, write it phonetically inside the editor. For instance, write "APSC" as "Ay-Pee-Es-See" or use the regional script to find the perfect spoken delivery. - **conversational Script Formatting:** Avoid direct textbook translations. Write exactly how you speak with your friends at a local tea stall. For a deeper understanding of this process, read our detailed tutorial on how to write scripts for natural text to speech.
