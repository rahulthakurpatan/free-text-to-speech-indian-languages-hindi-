3. What Makes a Good Assamese Voice Over

If you use a bad text-to-speech tool, your viewers will swipe away in two seconds. What separates a bad voice from a good voice?

Clarity: The words must be easy to understand on a cheap mobile phone speaker.
Pacing: It cannot sound like a breathless machine gun. It needs to pause slightly at commas and full stops.
Pronunciation: It must handle local Assamese names and places correctly without a heavy, weird Hindi accent.
Energy: For Shorts and Reels, the voice cannot be sleepy. It needs a firm, confident tone that holds attention.

6. Voice Quality Testing Framework

How do you know if your AI voice sounds good? Do the **Earphone Test**.

Generate a 10-second audio file. Put on your earphones. Close your eyes and listen.
• Did the voice take a breath at the end of the sentence?
• Did it emphasize the most important fact?
• Does it sound like a person talking to you, or a robot reading an encyclopaedia?

If it sounds bad, do not blame the tool immediately. Blame your script. Add commas (,) to force pauses. Change the spelling of complex Assamese words to English phonetics (spelling them out exactly how they sound to a local ear). This script engineering is vital, as discussed deeply in our Assamese Shorts Beginner Guide.

7. YouTube Shorts Voice Over Workflow

YouTube Shorts audiences are brutal. They decide whether to watch your video in the first two seconds.

Your workflow here must focus on the **Hook**. In your script, the very first sentence the AI voice speaks must be a shocking fact or a direct question. Example: "Did you know that Majuli is slowly disappearing?"

Once you generate the audio, import it into CapCut. You MUST use auto-captions (subtitles). Make the subtitles bold, place them in the middle of the screen, and use a kinetic animation (where the word turns yellow right as the voice says it). This visual lock prevents the user from scrolling.

8. Facebook Reels Voice Over Workflow

Facebook audiences behave slightly differently. They are usually slightly older and value community, nostalgia, and helpful daily information.

For Facebook, your AI voice over should be slightly slower and more conversational. Scripts explaining government schemes, local agricultural tips, or respectful stories about local temples perform exceptionally well here. The visual editing does not need to be as frantic as YouTube; a calm, clear presentation of facts builds high sharing behavior (the "Share" button is the most powerful tool on Facebook).

9. Instagram Reels Voice Over Workflow

Instagram is a highly aesthetic platform. The visual quality of your video matters heavily.

When using an Assamese voice over for Instagram, the audio must be perfectly synchronized with fast, high-quality visual cuts. Travel facts, food reviews, and quick motivational quotes perform best. Ensure that you place a trending instrumental music track in the background of your Reel, but turn the music volume down to 5%. This satisfies the Instagram algorithm (which likes trending audio) while allowing your AI voice over to be heard clearly.

10. How Narration Impacts Retention

"Retention" simply means how long someone watches your video before leaving.

If your AI voice speaks a 60-word paragraph without a single pause, the viewer's brain feels exhausted. They scroll away. If your AI voice pauses slightly after every important point, it creates a rhythm. The viewer's brain digests the fact, looks at the picture on the screen, and waits for the next sentence. By adding commas to your script, you are literally controlling the viewer's attention span.

11. Writing Better Reels Scripts

A 60-second video is roughly 120 to 140 words. You do not have time for long introductions.

Bad Script: "Hello friends, welcome back to my channel. Today in this short video I want to tell you some interesting facts about Assam tea that you might not know..." (10 seconds wasted. The viewer is gone).

Good Script: "Assam tea is so strong, it actually changed world history. Here are 3 reasons why..." (4 seconds. The viewer is hooked).

12. Common Voice Over Mistakes Beginners Make

Avoid these traps that destroy channel growth:

  • Using Google Translate Audio: This voice is meant for dictionaries, not storytelling. It is flat and boring. Use a proper neural AI generator.
  • Forgetting Subtitles: Many people watch Reels on buses with the sound off. If you do not have big subtitles on the screen, your AI voice over is useless.
  • Background Music Too Loud: If the viewer cannot hear the facts because your dramatic background music is blasting, they will swipe away in frustration. Keep music volume under 10%.

Short-Form Creator FAQ

Which is better for AI Shorts: YouTube or Instagram?

Both serve different purposes. YouTube Shorts are excellent for factual, educational, and historical content, and offer clear long-term monetization. Instagram Reels are better for travel, food, and highly visual content, and offer great opportunities for local brand sponsorships. Upload your content to both platforms to maximize reach.

How do I fix mispronounced words in Assamese?

The most powerful trick is phonetic spelling. If the AI tool mispronounces an Assamese town name when written in standard English, change the spelling in your script. Exaggerate the vowels or break the word apart until the generated audio sounds correct to a local ear.

Can I use copyrighted videos if my AI voice is original?

No. Even if your script and voice over are 100% original, using unedited clips from Bollywood movies or major news channels will trigger copyright strikes. Always use free stock footage (from Pexels or Pixabay) or images that fall under fair use educational commentary.
