3. What Makes a "Good" News AI Voice?

Psychologically, listeners trust voices that exhibit:

  • Emotional Restraint: A news voice shouldn't sound too happy or too sad. It needs to be neutral.
  • Clarity: Pronunciation of names like 'Mukhya Mantri' or 'Bhashan' must be sharp.
  • Confidence: The AI shouldn't trail off at the end of a sentence.
