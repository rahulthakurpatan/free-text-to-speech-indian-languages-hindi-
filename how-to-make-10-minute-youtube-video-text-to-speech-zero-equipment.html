STEP 1: Picking a Topic That Retains

Yar, a 10-minute video is a long time. If your topic is boring, no AI voice will save it. For beginners, I recommend niches where **Information is the Hero**.

  • Ancient History: ISRO’s secret missions or Indian kings.
  • Horror Stories: Deep narrative loops that keep people hooked at night.
  • Space/Science: Educational explainers with NASA stock footage.
  • Philosophy: Stoicism or Bhagavad Gita quotes for passive listening.

STEP 2: The Script is Your Soul

A 10-minute script should not feel like an essay. It must be conversational. - **The Hook (0-30s):** Start with "Did you know?" or a shocking fact. - **Open Loops:** Tell them what's coming at the end of the video. - **Short Sentences:** Long sentences sound robotic when read by AI.

When you write viral scripts, use punctuation as breath markers. This is the difference between a bot and a persona.

STEP 4: Visuals with Zero Budget

Since you have no camera, you must use **Stock Assets**. - **Pexels/Storyblocks:** For free high-quality video clips. - **Canva:** To animate static images or create overlays. - **Midjourney/Leonardo:** For unique AI-generated visuals for your story.

STEP 5: Mobile-First Editing

You don't need a high-end PC.
