Privacy Policy.

Last Updated: November 3, 2026

Privacy at a Glance

Vāṇī AI is a browser-based text-to-speech interface. You use your own Google API key (BYOK) to request audio generation. The website may use analytics, advertising, hosting, and notification providers. Your text and key are sent to Google when you choose to generate audio; please do not submit sensitive or confidential information.

Last updated: November 3, 2026

1. Who We Are

This Privacy Policy applies to Vāṇī AI, available at https://freetexttovoiceai.in/ (the “Website”). Vāṇī AI provides browser-based tools and educational content that help users convert text into speech using third-party AI services. The Website is operated by Vāṇī Studio (“we,” “us,” or “our”).

This policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used and shared, your choices, and how to contact us. It should be read with our Terms.

2. How the Text-to-Speech Tool Works

Vāṇī AI uses a Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) workflow. You provide your own Google API key and initiate audio generation from your browser. When you click Generate, the text, selected model/voice settings, and API key needed for the request are transmitted from your browser to Google’s API. Google processes that request under its own terms and privacy documentation.

  • We do not operate a Vāṇī AI server-side TTS account that receives every generation request.
  • We do not intentionally collect or store your submitted script or generated audio in a Vāṇī AI database.
  • However, the browser, Google, hosting/CDN services, extensions, and security software may process or retain information according to their own practices.
  • Do not enter passwords, financial information, health information, confidential business material, or other sensitive personal data into the text editor.

Our architecture and features may change. This policy describes the current intended workflow, not a guarantee that no third party ever processes request data.

3. API Keys and Browser Storage

The Website may store your API key in your browser’s local storage so you do not need to enter it again on every visit. Local storage is on the device and browser profile you use; it is not a secure vault. Anyone with access to that browser profile, malicious extensions, or compromised device may be able to access it. Website scripts running on this origin may also access local storage.

  • We do not intentionally transmit your key to Vāṇī AI servers for storage.
  • Your key is sent to Google when you use it to make an API request.
  • You can remove the saved key using the Studio reset/clear control if available, or clear this Website’s site data in your browser.
  • For stronger protection, use a restricted API key, set appropriate API restrictions and quotas in Google Cloud, and do not reuse a key with broad permissions.

We cannot guarantee that a key is protected from device compromise, browser extensions, or every client-side security risk. If you believe your key was exposed, revoke or rotate it through Google.

4. Information We May Collect

Depending on your browser, settings, and the services enabled, we and our providers may receive:

  • Technical and log information: IP address, request date/time, requested URL, referrer, browser and device details, operating system, and security or error logs.
  • Usage and analytics information: pages viewed, approximate engagement, language page visited, events, and general device or geographic information derived from network data.
  • Cookie and identifier information: cookies, local storage, or similar technologies used by analytics, advertising, preferences, and notification services.
  • Messages you send us: information you include when contacting support, such as your email address and message.
  • Notification choices: if you subscribe to browser notifications, the notification provider may process subscription identifiers and delivery information.

We do not require an account, name, or email address to use the core tool. Do not assume that visiting anonymously means no technical information is processed.

5. Cookies, Analytics, and Similar Technologies

Cookies and similar technologies may be used to make the Website work, remember preferences, measure traffic, prevent abuse, and support advertising. Some technologies are set by third-party providers whose services appear on our pages.

We use Google Analytics (measurement ID G-RV64RB8M6S) to understand Website usage and improve pages. Google Analytics may use cookies, device identifiers, and other information. Depending on implementation and settings, analytics data may include online identifiers and approximate location derived from IP address.

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect certain features. Browser-level controls do not necessarily stop all forms of measurement or local storage; use any consent and privacy controls shown on the Website where available.

We may display advertisements through Google AdSense to support the Website. Google and other advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, or similar identifiers to serve, measure, limit frequency, and personalize ads, including based on your visits to this Website or other websites, subject to your choices, applicable law, and Google’s settings.

Google’s advertising cookies enable Google and its partners to serve ads based on visits to our site and/or other sites. You can review or change personalized advertising choices in Google Ads Settings. You may also visit YourAdChoices for additional opt-out information. These controls may not disable all ads; ads may still be shown in non-personalized or contextual form.

7. Third-Party Providers

The Website relies on providers that may process information when you access or use it:

  • Google (Gemini/API, Analytics, AdSense, fonts, and related services): receives information required for API requests, analytics, or advertising. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

We do not sell personal information for money. We may disclose information to service providers who help operate the Website, when required by law, to protect rights and security, or in connection with a business transfer. We do not control third-party processing once information is sent to those providers.

Privacy FAQ

Does Vāṇī AI store my API key?

The current tool may save it in your browser’s local storage. We do not intentionally store it in a Vāṇī AI server database. Google receives it with API requests.

Does Vāṇī AI see my script?

Our client-side workflow is designed not to route scripts through a Vāṇī AI backend. Your script is sent to Google when you generate audio, and browser or third-party services may process technical information.

Does Google process my text?

Yes. Google processes the text and request information needed to generate audio. Review Google’s terms and privacy documentation for its handling and retention.

Does the Website use cookies?

Analytics, advertising, and other enabled third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies. Browser controls and any on-site consent controls can help manage them.

Can I opt out of personalized ads?

You can manage personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings and review other opt-out resources. Non-personalized ads may still appear.

How do I delete my API key?

Use the Studio reset control if available, or clear site storage in your browser. Also revoke or rotate the key in Google Cloud if it may have been exposed.

Do I need an account?

The core tool currently does not require a Vāṇī AI account. Third-party services may have their own account requirements.

How do I request access or deletion?

Contact us through the Website’s Contact page. We will review requests under applicable law and may need to verify identity or clarify the request.

We aim to minimize the information we collect and explain the third-party services used by Vāṇī AI. Please review this policy and the linked provider policies before using the tool.
