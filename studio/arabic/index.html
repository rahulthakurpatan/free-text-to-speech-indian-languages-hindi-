ARABIC TEXT TO SPEECH GUIDE

Create clearer Arabic AI voiceovers

Use the script and delivery style that fit your audience. Arabic TTS can read formal narration, creator scripts and lessons, but dialects and pronunciation can vary. Generate a short test before producing a long recording.

01

Use Arabic script and punctuation

Paste Arabic Unicode text directly. The editor supports right-to-left entry and display. Keep punctuation and sentence breaks clear to help shape pauses.

02

Add diacritics when they help

Arabic often omits short-vowel marks in everyday writing. Add tashkeel to names or ambiguous words when pronunciation matters, then listen to confirm the result.

03

Check dialect and code-switching

Arabic varies across regions. This studio does not promise a specific Egyptian, Gulf, Levantine or Maghrebi dialect. Review regional vocabulary and English-Arabic phrases in the audio.

Modern Standard Arabic and regional speech

Modern Standard Arabic is a practical default for lessons, explainers and narration intended for broad audiences. Everyday speech differs across Arabic-speaking regions. Use the voice direction as guidance, but verify that the output suits the audience you have in mind.

Make Arabic narration easier to follow

Break long scripts into short paragraphs, spell out abbreviations that may be read unexpectedly and test personal names separately. A fluent Arabic speaker should review pronunciation when accuracy is important.

Use cases for an Arabic AI voice generator

Create draft narration for YouTube videos, social clips, educational lessons, stories, accessibility listening and creator explainers. Preview the voice and check applicable usage rights before publishing.

Bring your own Gemini API key

Vani does not provide API credits. The key is sent from your browser to Google's Gemini API. Google controls model availability, quotas, terms and any applicable charges. Remove a saved key when you no longer need it, and avoid saving it on a shared device.

GOOD TO KNOW

Arabic text to speech FAQs

How do I convert Arabic text to speech?

Paste Arabic Unicode text in the right-to-left script box, add your Gemini API key, choose a voice and narration direction, then generate and preview the audio.

Does the studio support Arabic script and right-to-left typing?

Yes. The Arabic script field is set up for right-to-left Arabic Unicode input. You can include diacritics when they help clarify pronunciation.

Which Arabic dialect does Gemini use?

Arabic is supported by Gemini TTS, but this studio does not guarantee a specific dialect. Modern Standard Arabic is the recommended starting point; review regional expressions and pronunciation in the generated audio.

Can I use this for Arabic YouTube videos and lessons?

You can generate narration for creator videos, lessons, stories and other projects. Review usage rights and Google API terms for your use case.

Does Vani provide Gemini API credits?

No. This is a bring-your-own-key studio. Google controls model access, quota, terms and any applicable charges.

Can I download Arabic speech as MP3?

After generation succeeds, preview the audio and choose Download MP3.
