MARATHI TTS GUIDE

How to create a clearer Marathi voiceover

Marathi text-to-speech quality depends on Marathi spelling, punctuation, local vocabulary, forms of address and how the text is written for speech. Generate a short sample first, listen carefully, then adjust your script or delivery direction before creating a longer narration.

01

Use Marathi Devanagari Unicode text

Paste Marathi Devanagari Unicode text in Devanagari and check that the characters display correctly. Text copied from legacy fonts may need conversion before you generate audio.

02

Write for spoken Marathi

Use short, spoken Marathi sentences and natural paragraph breaks. Keep Marathi forms of address and local expressions as intended. Spell out names, dates or numbers if the voice reads them unclearly.

03

Review local words and names

Listen for place names, personal names, dates, amounts and Marathi-English code-switching. Ask a fluent Marathi speaker to review the audio when pronunciation matters.

Marathi script and pronunciation

Marathi is commonly written in the Balbodh variant of Devanagari. Review pronunciation of Marathi-specific sounds such as ळ, as well as names, numbers and English words mixed into the script. A voice model may pronounce local words differently, so listen before publishing.

A language with a global audience

India’s 2011 Census recorded 83,026,680 people who returned Marathi as their mother tongue. Marathi-speaking communities also live around the world. This audience-size figure is historical Census data, not a current population estimate. View the Census language table ↗

Marathi and Hindi use Devanagari

Both Marathi and Hindi use Devanagari, but Marathi has distinct spelling and pronunciation conventions. This studio accepts Marathi Devanagari Unicode text. Romanized Marathi input has not been tested; use Devanagari for the most reliable results.

Marathi is its own language

Marathi and Hindi are different languages, even though both are commonly written in Devanagari. Enter Marathi wording rather than Hindi text. Review local words, names and mixed-language phrases in the generated audio before publishing.

Marathi narration for stories, lessons and videos

Marathi text to speech can help creators prepare story narration, cultural explainers, lessons and short videos. Match the delivery to the use: a story may need a warm pace, while a classroom lesson or public announcement may need a clearer, measured read. Start with a short passage, listen, then adjust your wording or voice direction before generating a longer script.

Check place names, personal names, Marathi forms of address, dates, amounts and English words mixed into the script. Marathi-speaking audiences in Maharashtra and the wider diaspora may use different local expressions and conventions. If the exact pronunciation or regional style matters, ask a fluent Marathi listener to review the audio.

Write your script before generating, keep each paragraph focused on one idea and use the optional voice direction to suggest mood or pacing. This studio reads the Marathi text you provide; it does not translate English or Hindi. For accessibility or learning materials, review the full audio and make sure the spoken version preserves the meaning and structure of the original text.

GOOD TO KNOW

Marathi text to speech FAQs

How do I create Marathi text to speech?

Paste Marathi Devanagari Unicode text, add your Gemini API key, choose a voice and delivery direction, then select Generate Marathi voice.

Does this studio translate English or Hindi into Marathi?

No. Enter the Marathi wording you want spoken. This studio sends your supplied script to Gemini TTS; it is not a translation tool.

Is Vani Studio free?

Vani provides the studio without bundled Google API credits. You bring your own Gemini API key; Google controls quota, terms and any applicable charges.

Where is my Gemini API key stored?

When Remember on this device is checked, the key is stored in this browser's local storage until removed. It is sent directly to Google's Gemini API for generation.

Can I download Marathi voice audio as MP3?

After generation succeeds, preview the audio and use Download MP3. Listen through the result before publishing it.

Does Gemini guarantee a specific Marathi regional accent?

No. Gemini language support does not guarantee a specific Marathi regional accent or perfect pronunciation of every name. Review the output with a fluent Marathi speaker when accuracy matters.

Does this studio support Romanized Marathi input?

The studio is designed for Marathi Devanagari Unicode text. Romanized Marathi input has not been tested, so use Devanagari for the most reliable results.
