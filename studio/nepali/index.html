NEPALI TTS GUIDE

How to create a clearer Nepali voiceover

Nepali text-to-speech quality depends on Devanagari spelling, punctuation, respectful forms of address and how the script is written for speech. Generate a short sample first, listen carefully, then adjust your script or delivery direction before creating a longer narration.

01

Use Devanagari Unicode text

Paste Nepali text written in Devanagari Unicode and check that the characters display correctly. Text copied from legacy fonts may need conversion before you generate audio.

02

Write for spoken Nepali

Use short Nepali sentences, natural paragraph breaks and familiar punctuation. Spell out names or numbers if the voice reads them unclearly.

03

Review local words and names

Listen for place names, personal names, dates, amounts and Nepali-English code-switches. Ask a fluent Nepali speaker to review the audio when pronunciation matters.

Devanagari input and Romanized Nepali

Nepali is written in Devanagari. This studio expects Nepali Unicode text in Devanagari; it does not transliterate Romanized Nepali or translate Hindi and English. Hindi and Nepali share a script but remain separate languages, so choose the Nepali wording you want spoken.

What Gemini language support means

Google lists Nepali among Gemini TTS supported languages. Language support does not guarantee a specific regional accent or perfect pronunciation of every name. Generate a short passage and ask a fluent Nepali speaker to review it when accuracy matters.

Nepali narration for stories, lessons and videos

Start with the audience and purpose. A story may need a warm, paced delivery; a lesson or announcement may need a clear, measured read. Use short paragraphs and natural punctuation to help the narration follow the structure. Preview a short passage before generating a longer script.

Check place names, personal names, Nepali honorifics, dates, amounts and English words mixed into the script. Nepal and Nepali-speaking communities outside Nepal may use different local expressions and conventions. If the exact pronunciation or regional style matters, ask a fluent Nepali listener to review the audio.

Write your script before generating, keep each paragraph focused on one idea and use the optional voice direction to suggest mood or pacing. This studio reads the Nepali text you provide; it does not translate English or Hindi. For accessibility or learning materials, review the full audio and make sure the spoken version preserves the meaning and structure of the original text.

GOOD TO KNOW

Nepali text to speech FAQs

How do I create Nepali text to speech?

Paste Nepali text written in Devanagari Unicode, add your Gemini API key, choose a voice and delivery direction, then select Generate Nepali voice.

Does this studio translate English or Hindi into Nepali?

No. Enter the Nepali wording you want spoken. This studio sends your supplied script to Gemini TTS; it is not a translation tool.

Is Vani Studio free?

Vani provides the studio without bundled Google API credits. You bring your own Gemini API key; Google controls quota, terms and any applicable charges.

Where is my Gemini API key stored?

When Remember on this device is checked, the key is stored in this browser's local storage until removed. It is sent directly to Google's Gemini API for generation.

Can I download Nepali voice audio as MP3?

After generation succeeds, preview the audio and use Download MP3. Listen through the result before publishing it.

Does Gemini guarantee a specific Nepali regional accent?

No. Gemini language support does not guarantee a specific Nepali regional accent or perfect pronunciation of every name. Review the output with a fluent Nepali speaker when accuracy matters.

Does this studio convert Romanized Nepali to Devanagari?

No. Paste Nepali Unicode text written in Devanagari. Romanized Nepali is not automatically transliterated.
