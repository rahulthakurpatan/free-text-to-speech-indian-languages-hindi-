ODIA TTS GUIDE

How to create a clearer Odia voiceover

Odia text-to-speech quality depends on spelling, punctuation, names and how the script is written for speaking. Generate a short sample first, listen carefully, then adjust your script or delivery direction before creating a longer narration.

01

Use Odia Unicode text

Paste Odia Unicode text and check that the Odia characters display correctly. Text copied from legacy non-Unicode fonts may need conversion before you generate audio.

02

Write for spoken Odia

Use concise sentences, natural paragraph breaks and the Odia sentence mark (।) where appropriate. Spell out names or numbers that the voice does not read clearly.

03

Review local words and names

Listen for place names, personal names, numbers and borrowed words. Ask a fluent Odia speaker to review the finished audio when pronunciation matters.

Odia and the older spelling Oriya

Odia is the current official spelling in India; Oriya is the older English spelling for the same language. This studio is for Odia-language scripts, not translation from Hindi or English.

What Gemini language support means

Google lists Odia as supported by Gemini TTS. Language support does not guarantee a particular Odisha regional accent or perfect pronunciation of every name. Test a short passage and review the audio before publishing.

A quick review before publishing

Listen through the full output once. Note unclear spellings, local expressions, numerals and mixed-language terms. Revise the lines that need attention, generate a short sample and compare it with your script. If regional accuracy matters, ask a fluent listener from your intended audience to review it.

Write your script before generating, keep each paragraph focused on one idea and use the optional voice direction to suggest mood or pacing. This studio reads the Odia text you provide; it does not translate English or Hindi. Check text and platform rights before publishing the audio.

GOOD TO KNOW

Odia Studio FAQs

How do I create Odia text to speech?

Paste Odia Unicode text, add your Gemini API key, choose a voice and delivery direction, then select Generate Odia voice.

Does this studio translate English or Hindi into Odia?

No. Enter the Odia wording you want spoken. This studio sends your supplied script to Gemini TTS; it is not a translation tool.

Is Vani Studio free?

Vani provides the studio without bundled Google API credits. You bring your own Gemini API key; Google controls quota, terms and any applicable charges.

Where is my Gemini API key stored?

When Remember on this device is checked, the key is stored in this browser's local storage until removed. It is sent directly to Google's Gemini API for generation.

Can I download Odia voice audio as MP3?

After generation succeeds, preview the audio and use Download MP3. Listen through the result before publishing it.

Does Gemini guarantee a specific Odia regional accent?

No. Odia language support does not guarantee a particular regional accent or perfect pronunciation of every name. Review the audio with a fluent Odia speaker when accuracy matters.

Is Odia the same as Oriya?

Odia is the current official spelling; Oriya is the older spelling for the same language. Use Odia Unicode text in this studio.
